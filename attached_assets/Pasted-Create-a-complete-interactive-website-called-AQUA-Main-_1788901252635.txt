==================================================
THE CONCEPT
==================================================

AQUA is an interactive digital aquarium and aquarium planning platform.

It combines:

1. An immersive underwater exploration experience.
2. A catalog of aquarium animals.
3. Detailed care and habitat requirements.
4. Aquarium setup recommendations.
5. Fish and animal compatibility checking.
6. Aquarium building and planning.
7. Aquarium tanks, equipment and supplies.
8. Kuwait store and price comparison.
9. Complete habitat/setup cost estimates.

The goal is NOT simply to sell fish.

The website should help users understand:

“What does this animal actually need before I buy it?”

The experience should encourage responsible aquarium ownership.
