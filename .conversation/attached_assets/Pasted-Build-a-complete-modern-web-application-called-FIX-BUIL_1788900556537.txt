--------------------------------------------------
CORE IDEA
--------------------------------------------------

FIX & BUILD helps users do three things:

1. FIX IT
Diagnose something broken and understand what is needed to repair it.

2. BUILD IT
Describe something they want to build and receive a project plan with suggested materials, tools, and steps.

3. STUDIO
A dedicated mode for architecture and design students to plan physical models, presentation projects, prototypes, and material-based university work.

For now, the website can use realistic demo data.
Do NOT create fake live integrations.

--------------------------------------------------
VISUAL STYLE
--------------------------------------------------

The design must feel completely different from:
- luxury fashion websites
- AYLA style
- classic/elegant layouts
- navy or black tech websites
- AI/robot interfaces
- gaming websites
- corporate construction websites

Visual direction:

SOFT WORKSHOP / CREATIVE MAKER STUDIO

Use:
- warm milky cream
- warm off-white
- soft butter yellow
- muted sage green
- dusty coral
- powder blue

Keep the palette soft, clean, modern and creative.

DO NOT use:
- navy blue
- black futuristic backgrounds
- neon green
- purple AI gradients
- heavy glassmorphism
- luxury serif typography
- generic SaaS dashboard layouts
- excessive 3D
- boring card grids

Typography:
Use a bold but friendly rounded sans-serif font.

Use small handwritten-style annotations only for:
- measurements
- arrows
- labels
- notes
- material callouts

The interface should feel like:
a modern workshop table +
an architectural sketchbook +
a clean creative studio.

Use irregular but intentional layouts.

Design elements can include:
- measuring tape
- screws
- paint can
- ruler
- wood pieces
- pencil
- paper layers
- tape
- architectural measurement marks
- sketch-style arrows
- tool silhouettes

These objects should feel integrated into the page.

--------------------------------------------------
MOTION
--------------------------------------------------

Examples:
- measuring tape extends slightly
- screw rotates on hover
- wood pieces align
- measurement lines animate in
- materials slide onto a virtual workbench
- an object visually repairs itself

Do not make the experience heavily 3D.
