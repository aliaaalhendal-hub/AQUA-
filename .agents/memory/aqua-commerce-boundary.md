---
name: AQUA commerce boundary
description: Defines what AQUA may charge through Stripe versus what remains third-party comparison data.
---

Keep third-party Kuwait retailer listings comparison-only. AQUA's Stripe checkout is currently limited to a clearly labeled test-only sample product; it must not charge for retailer listings without an authorized ordering and fulfillment arrangement.

**Why:** Charging third-party listings through AQUA would make AQUA responsible for inventory, fulfillment, refunds, and delivery without a retailer commerce connection.

**How to apply:** Preserve the separation in shop and checkout work. Before enabling real purchases, establish what AQUA owns or is authorized to sell and who fulfills each order.
